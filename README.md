# Attack on Titan: Utopia

*Attack on Titan: Utopia* is a single-player, endless tower defense game inspired by the popular anime *Attack on Titan*. Players defend the Utopia District, located on the northern border of Wall Rose, from relentless titan invasions. The objective is to strategically deploy various Anti-Titan weapons to prevent the titans from breaching the defenses and protect humanity’s last stronghold.

## Game Synopsis

Set in a post-apocalyptic world where humanity is protected by three massive walls—Wall Maria, Wall Rose, and Wall Sina—players face the aftermath of Wall Maria’s breach. The titans now threaten Wall Rose, and the Utopia District stands as the final barrier. Players command the human forces to hold the line using a range of specialized weaponry.

## Core Features

- Endless tower defense gameplay with progressively challenging titan waves.  
- Diverse Anti-Titan arsenal, each with unique abilities and tactical advantages.  
- Resource management to deploy, upgrade, and position weapons for optimal defense.  
- Immersive experience drawing heavily from the *Attack on Titan* universe and storyline.

## Gameplay Mechanics

- Prevent titans from breaching Wall Rose by strategically placing and upgrading weapons in the Utopia District.  
- Earn in-game currency by defeating titans, which can be used for weapon deployment and enhancements.  
- Survive as many waves as possible to achieve high scores based on titan kills and waves cleared.  
- Use mouse or touch controls to select and position defensive weapons on the battlefield.
